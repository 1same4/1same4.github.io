About Ali Al-Janabi

Ali Al-Janabi is the founder of Janabi AI, a digital solutions and AI agency in Baghdad, Iraq. He started as a designer who went on to run a full print shop (2017), worked as a travel and tourism representative and tour leader (2019), and founded his own travel company (2021). Today he turns that field experience into AI-powered websites, systems, AI agents and project consulting, and teaches young people to use AI tools.
